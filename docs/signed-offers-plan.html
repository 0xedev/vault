Signed EIP-712 Offers

Submitting an offer signs typed data and does not lock USDC. Direct fixed-price buy still funds escrow immediately. Acceptance verifies the signed offer on-chain and then follows the correct product flow.

Core Flow

  • NFT loan offers are signed by lenders and accepted by borrowers on-chain.
  • P2P market offers are signed by buyers and accepted by sellers on-chain.
  • P2P accepted offers lock buyer USDC in escrow. Seller payout still waits for delivery and buyer confirmation.
  • Offer nonces can be cancelled on-chain to invalidate signatures.

Contracts

  • VaultNFT.acceptSignedOffer verifies a SignedLoanOffer, pulls USDC from lender, pays borrower minus platform fee, and activates the loan.
  • VaultDeals.acceptSignedDealOffer verifies a SignedDealOffer, pulls USDC from buyer into escrow, and moves the deal to funded delivery flow.
  • cancelOfferNonce and cancelOfferNonces mark signer nonces as unavailable.

API and Storage

  • offers stores offer_type, signature, nonce, expires_at, typed_data, marketplace, and contract_listing_id.
  • POST /api/offers rebuilds typed data server-side and verifies signatures with viem.
  • PATCH /api/offers requires a confirmed accept transaction hash for accepted offers and creates funded escrow rows for P2P markets.

Frontend

  • NFT detail submit-offer signs EIP-712 data instead of depositing USDC.
  • Mini Apps, X, Farcaster, Clanker, and Bundles expose signed offer submission beside direct escrow buy.
  • Profile shows incoming offers under active listings with Accept and Reject controls.
  • Profile shows outgoing offers with Cancel, using on-chain nonce cancellation where possible.
